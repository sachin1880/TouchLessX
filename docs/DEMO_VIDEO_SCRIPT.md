# Demo video script

A 3-minute screen recording that shows a new user every gesture working. Record it once
in the **Default** profile, so it matches the gesture table in the README.

## Before you record (5 minutes)

1. **Profile:** choose **Default** in the header.
2. **Window:** maximise TouchlessX. Turn on the **floating mini controller**
   (Settings → Appearance) so pausing is visible when other apps are in front.
3. **Desktop:** put a folder called `Demo` on the Desktop with one picture in it. Open
   in the background: Safari or Chrome on a long web page, Music or YouTube with a song
   ready, and Keynote/PowerPoint with three slides.
4. **Do Not Disturb:** turn it on (Control Centre → Focus), so no notification lands in
   the video.
5. **Light:** face a window or a lamp. Sit 50–70 cm from the camera, hand at chest
   height.
6. **Practise once:** go through **Learn** once before recording.

## Recording

- Press **⌘ ⇧ 5** → **Record Entire Screen** → Options → Microphone: *MacBook
  Microphone* (if you will talk over it) → **Record**. Stop from the menu bar.
- Or use QuickTime Player → File → New Screen Recording.
- Keep each gesture slow and clear, and pause for a second after it so viewers see the
  result. Mistakes are fine: cut them later.

## Shot list

| # | Time | On screen | Do | Say (optional) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | TouchlessX Dashboard | Wave at the camera; the preview draws the hand skeleton. | "This is TouchlessX. It turns your webcam into a mouse — everything runs on this computer." |
| 2 | 0:10 | Learn page | Click **Learn**, show step 1, press Next, do **Open Palm** until it says *Well done!* | "New users start with the Learn page. It teaches each gesture and checks you got it right." |
| 3 | 0:25 | Dashboard → Desktop | Minimise TouchlessX. **Open palm**: move the cursor to all four corners of the screen. | "Open palm moves the cursor." |
| 4 | 0:40 | Desktop | Move onto the `Demo` folder. **Pinch twice quickly** — the folder opens. Pinch twice on the picture to open it. | "Pinch is a click. Pinch twice to open a file or folder." |
| 5 | 0:55 | Desktop | **Two fingers** (V) on the picture — the right-click menu appears. Close it with a pinch outside it. | "Two fingers is a right click." |
| 6 | 1:05 | Browser on a long page | **Three fingers**, hold, move the hand down and up — the page scrolls. | "Three fingers and move to scroll." |
| 7 | 1:20 | Browser | **Both hands pinching**, move them apart (zoom in), then together (zoom out). | "Pinch with both hands and stretch to zoom." |
| 8 | 1:35 | Music / YouTube | **Fist**, hold a moment — playback pauses. Fist again — it plays. | "A fist plays and pauses." |
| 9 | 1:45 | Music / YouTube | **Point**, hold, move the finger up (louder) and down (quieter). Show the volume overlay. | "Point and move for volume." |
| 10 | 2:00 | Keynote / PowerPoint, playing | **Flick right** twice (next slide, next slide), **flick left** once (previous). | "Flick to change slides." |
| 11 | 2:20 | Any app | **Both palms** up, hold — the mini controller shows *Paused*; wave around: nothing happens. Both palms again — resumed. | "Both palms pause and resume, so your hands can rest." |
| 12 | 2:35 | TouchlessX | Show the red **EMERGENCY STOP** button and press **Space** to pause. | "Space, Esc or the emergency stop button stop it instantly." |
| 13 | 2:45 | Gesture Studio | Scroll the mapping list; change one gesture's action in the dropdown. | "Every gesture can be changed, and each profile keeps its own set." |
| 14 | 2:55 | Dashboard | End on the dashboard. | "TouchlessX — control your world with hand gestures." |

## After recording

1. **Trim:** open the recording in QuickTime → Edit → Trim (⌘ T) to cut the start and
   end, or iMovie to cut mistakes in the middle.
2. **Check privacy:** the video shows your face in the preview and your desktop. Close
   personal windows and use an empty Desktop if you will share it publicly.
3. **Share:** upload to YouTube (Unlisted is fine) and copy the link.
4. **Put it in the README:** under *Screenshots*, add a thumbnail that links to the video:

   ```markdown
   [![Watch the TouchlessX demo](docs/screenshots/dashboard.png)](https://youtu.be/YOUR_VIDEO_ID)
   ```

   GitHub can also play a short video directly: drag the `.mp4` (under 10 MB) into the
   README editor on github.com and it inserts a player.
